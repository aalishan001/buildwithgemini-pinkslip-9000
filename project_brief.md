# My agent: The AI Automation Apocalypse Dashboard (PinkSlip-9000)
One-liner: A satirical dashboard and analytics agent that tracks and visualizes AI-driven workforce layoffs across customizable regions and infinite time dials with a catalog of corporate downsizing excuses, doom-gauges, and AI replacement metrics.

Tool coverage:
- Memory: Remembers the user's current job title ("checking if you're next"), their cynicism index, tracked regions, and previously dialed scenarios across sessions.
- Tools: Look up layoff events filtered by region and time dials; extract corporate "synergy/efficiency" euphemisms; calculate satirical metrics like "GPUs Purchased per Worker Laid Off".
- Catalog/UI: Rich A2UI cards featuring "Corporate AI Excuse of the Week", layoff impact scorecards, company leaderboards, and comparison tables (e.g., human salaries saved vs. cloud inference bills).
- Image gen: Generates satirical "Certificate of Obsolescence" badges, robotic corporate executive avatars, and dystopian milestone awards.
- Sandbox: Executes Python scripts to crunch limitless dial ranges (sliding across regions and time periods), calculating rate-of-replacement curves, statistical regressions, and chart datasets.

Recommended for every project: memory, storage, tools, image generation, A2UI
Agent-specific / stretch (pick what fits): Code sandbox for limitless dial math and chart computations, Firestore for community-submitted layoff records, A2UI rich dashboard widgets.
